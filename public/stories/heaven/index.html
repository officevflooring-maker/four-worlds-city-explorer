Heavenly City: a story in three chapters

A visit to the holy city the Bible describes: an open gate, the river of life and a song from every nation. You are the young builder with the satchel and the toolbox.

Chapter 1 The Open Gate

In the Bible: The Bible says the twelve gates of the holy city are pearls, and that its gates will never be shut. Jesus promised his friends that he would prepare a place for them. Revelation 21:21–25; John 14:1–3

  • Pearl gate
  • House of many rooms
  • Welcome hall

Chapter 2 The River

In the Bible: The Bible describes the river of the water of life, clear as crystal, and the tree of life beside it, bearing fruit every month, with leaves for the healing of the nations. Revelation 22:1–2

  • Tree of life
  • Fountain of life
  • Garden of light

Chapter 3 The Song

In the Bible: The Bible describes a great crowd from every nation, tribe, people and language, standing together and praising God. Revelation 7:9–10

  • Harp workshop
  • Bell tower
  • Rainbow pavilion
