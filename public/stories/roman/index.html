Ancient Rome: a story in three chapters

A Roman town gets a new road, water for its fountains and a feast of thanks. You are the young builder with the satchel and the toolbox.

Chapter 1 The Road to the Hill

From real life: Roman roads were built in layers of earth, gravel and stone, and many ran almost straight for miles. Some are still used today.

  • City walls and towers
  • Ox cart
  • Warehouse (horrea)

Chapter 2 Water for the City

From real life: Aqueducts carried water downhill on a very gentle slope, sometimes from many miles away, to the city's fountains, baths and homes.

  • Aqueduct
  • Public fountain
  • Forum

Chapter 3 The Harvest Feast

From real life: Bread was the main food in Rome, and cities had many bakeries. In Pompeii, archaeologists found a bakery oven with loaves still inside.

  • Snack bar (thermopolium)
  • Farmhouse (villa rustica)
  • Olive grove
