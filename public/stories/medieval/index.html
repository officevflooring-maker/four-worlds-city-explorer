Medieval Kingdom: a story in three chapters

A castle town opens its old gate, saves its harvest and holds a tournament. You are the young builder with the satchel and the toolbox.

Chapter 1 The Old Castle

From real life: Real castle gates were guarded by a heavy grille called a portcullis, and the blacksmith made the iron for locks, hinges and tools.

  • Royal castle
  • Gatehouse
  • Blacksmith

Chapter 2 The Harvest Fair

From real life: Most medieval people were farmers, and a wet harvest could mean a hungry winter. Towns held a market every week, and some held a big fair once a year.

  • Farmstead
  • Windmill
  • Market hall

Chapter 3 The Tournament

From real life: At tournaments, knights jousted with blunted lances. In England, townsmen also practiced with the longbow at the archery butts.

  • Archery butts
