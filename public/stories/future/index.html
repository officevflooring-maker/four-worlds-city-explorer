Robotic Future: a story in three chapters

A young settler powers up a colony, rescues a lost rover and opens the first school. You are the young builder with the satchel and the toolbox.

Chapter 1 Power Up

From real life: On Mars, dust really does settle on solar panels. NASA's rovers Spirit and Opportunity lost power to dust, and gusts of wind sometimes blew their panels clean.

  • Power tower
  • Robot monument
  • Life support dome

Chapter 2 The Lost Rover

From real life: Radio messages take several minutes to travel between Earth and Mars, so rovers there have to look after themselves between orders.

  • Pressurized rover
  • Rover test track
  • Communications relay

Chapter 3 First Class

From real life: Astronauts on the International Space Station have taught lessons to schools on Earth by radio and video call.

  • Airlock and suit hangar
  • Hologram hall
