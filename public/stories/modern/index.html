Modern Town: a story in three chapters

An empty lot by the river becomes a town, with a new bridge and a library full of light. You are the young builder with the satchel and the toolbox.

Chapter 1 A Fresh Start

From real life: Before a new building goes up, the town checks its plans, so it will be safe and fit in with its neighbors.

  • Building materials
  • Tower crane
  • Family house

Chapter 2 The Bridge

From real life: Bridges, sidewalks and parks belong to everyone. Towns pay for them with taxes and plan safe ways for kids to reach them.

  • Streets and crosswalks
  • City park
  • Treehouse playground

Chapter 3 Lights On

From real life: A public library is free for everyone. Electricians check its wiring, because old wires can wear out and become unsafe.

  • Public library
  • School
  • Science center and observatory
