Ancient Egypt: a story in three chapters

A village on the Nile, from the yearly flood to a giant stone on the move. You are the young builder with the satchel and the toolbox.

Chapter 1 The Flood

From real life: The real Nile flooded every summer and left rich black soil behind. The Egyptians called their country Kemet, the black land.

  • Granary
  • Lifting lever
  • Palm grove

Chapter 2 The Scribe's Lesson

From real life: Scribes wrote with reed pens on papyrus, a paper made from a reed that grew along the Nile. Few people could read, so scribes counted the harvests and the taxes.

  • Scribe school
  • House of Life
  • Great Library

Chapter 3 The Great Stone

From real life: A painting in an Egyptian tomb shows a giant statue on a sled, with a worker pouring water on the sand in front of it. Wet sand is much easier to drag a sled over than dry sand.

  • Stone sledge
  • Pyramid
  • Temple gateway
