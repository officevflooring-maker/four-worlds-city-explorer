Questions

What grades is it for?

Grades 2 to 8. The definitions are short and written for children; older students can use the glossary for research.

Does it go with the Classroom Pack?

Yes. The words come from the same places as the Classroom Pack's fact cards and worksheets, so the two work well together.

Can I copy it for my students?

Yes, as many copies as your class needs. One purchase is for one teacher's classroom or one home; please don't share the file online.
