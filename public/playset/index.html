How it works

  1. 1Print and tape the mat. Four ordinary pages make one big mat with a district for every world.
  2. 2Cut and fold. Each stand-up folds into a little tent with its picture on both sides. Card stock makes them sturdy.
  3. 3Build and play. Build each world, mix them together, and tell the story of the people who live there.

Questions

What age is it for?

Ages 4 and up with a grown-up to help cut; children from about 7 can make it themselves.

What do I need?

Scissors, tape, and card stock for the stand-ups if you have it. Plain paper works for the mat.

Can I print more?

Yes, as often as you like for your own home or classroom. Please don't share the file online.
