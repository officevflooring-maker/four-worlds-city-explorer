Questions

Can young children really play?

Yes. Level 1 only asks them to name the place in a picture, and the name is often something they will have seen on this site. A grown-up reads the card.

Are the questions about real places?

Each question is about a place on The Atlas of Cities: real ones from Egypt, Rome and today, and imagined ones from the elven, future and heavenly worlds. Every answer is explained on the site.

What do I need?

Scissors, a pencil, and thick paper if you have it. Cards last longer on card stock or in sleeves.

Can I print more copies?

Yes, for your own home or classroom. Please don't share the file online.
