Elven Green World · People

People of the Elven World

Imagined The elves are imagined. Their roles, though, are real ones every community needs: elders, gardeners, makers, keepers of knowledge and protectors.

  • Elder

    Keeper of memory

    Elders remember how the forest has changed over many years. In the real world, long-term observations, such as records of when blossoms open, help scientists track climate change.

    Where to find them: Seasons tower, Council terrace, Seed library

    Find them on the map

  • Forest ranger

    Watcher of the woods

    Rangers know every path and animal. Real rangers protect wildlife, guide visitors and help prevent wildfires.

    Where to find them: Tiny forest, Stag crossing, Canopy climbing course

    Find them on the map

  • Young elf

    Learning outdoors

    Young elves learn in the forest itself. Real forest schools teach children outdoors in all weather.

    Where to find them: Forest school, Pollinator meadow, Forest training grove

    Find them on the map

  • Crystal keeper

    Scholar with a glowing staff

    In this imagined world, crystals glow with light. Real scientists are studying plants and fungi that glow, and how to store energy from the sun.

    Where to find them: Glowing tower, Star dome, Seed library

    Find them on the map

  • Gardener

    Watering the plants

    Gardeners keep the city green and fed. Real community gardeners grow food, compost waste and support bees.

    Where to find them: Rain garden court, Pollinator meadow, Harvest market hall

    Find them on the map · Join their tour: Ecological design

  • Guardian

    Protector with a leaf shield

    Guardians protect the forest and its people. Every community needs people who keep others safe.

    Where to find them: Palace of the long future, Council terrace, Deer shrine

    Find them on the map

  • Woodworker

    Carrying a bundle of wood

    Woodworkers use fallen and carefully chosen timber, and repair things instead of throwing them away.

    Where to find them: Repair workshop, Timber family house, Living crane

    Find them on the map

  • Crystal gatherer

    Carrying glowing crystals

    An imagined job. In the real world, miners and recyclers recover the metals and minerals that technology depends on.

    Where to find them: Cleansing fountain, Crystal waterfall, Harvest market hall

    Find them on the map
