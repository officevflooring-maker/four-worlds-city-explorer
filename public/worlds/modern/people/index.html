Modern Town · People

People of the Modern Town

Real world A town has to work for everyone: children walking to school, runners, older people, dog walkers and workers. Each person uses the same streets in a different way.

  • Neighbor out walking

    Walking to the shops

    Walking is the oldest way to get around, and every trip starts and ends on foot. Wide sidewalks, shade trees and short crossings make walking easier and safer.

    Where to find them: Streets and crosswalks, Shops with homes above, Corner store

    Find them on the map

  • Office worker

    Commuting with a briefcase

    Many people travel into the center each day for work. How they get there, by car, bus, train, bike or on foot, shapes traffic and air quality.

    Where to find them: Office building, Skyscraper, Taxi

    Find them on the map

  • Teen with a backpack

    On the way to school

    Children and teens can't drive, so they depend on safe walking and cycling routes, buses and nearby schools, parks and libraries.

    Where to find them: School, Public library, Recreation center

    Find them on the map · Join their tour: Modern neighborhoods

  • Runner

    Out for exercise

    Parks, paths and sports fields make it easy for people to stay active close to home.

    Where to find them: City park, Sports field, Recreation center

    Find them on the map

  • Older neighbor

    Walking with a cane

    Benches, curb ramps, even sidewalks and long enough crossing times help older people stay independent. Good design for them helps everyone.

    Where to find them: Health clinic, Community center, Streets and crosswalks

    Find them on the map

  • Dog walker

    Walking the dog

    Dog walkers are out in every season and often know the neighborhood best. Parks and green spaces matter to them and their dogs.

    Where to find them: City park, Community garden, Treehouse playground

    Find them on the map

  • Construction worker

    Carrying supplies

    Builders wear hard hats and high-visibility vests to stay safe on site.

    Where to find them: Tower crane, Building materials, Pickup truck

    Find them on the map

  • Carpenter

    Carrying lumber

    Carpenters frame houses, build stairs and fit doors and windows.

    Where to find them: Family house, Building materials, Warehouse

    Find them on the map
