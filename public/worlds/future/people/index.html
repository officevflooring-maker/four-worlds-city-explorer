Robotic Future · People

People and robots of the Future

Possible future An imagined Moon and Mars settlement. Every person has an important job, and robots do the dull, dirty and dangerous work. The jobs are based on real space careers.

  • Settler

    In a light pressure suit

    Settlers would need suits whenever they went outside. Real spacesuits provide air, pressure, warmth and protection from dust and radiation.

    Where to find them: Airlock and suit hangar, Life support dome, Moon base home

  • Engineer

    In an orange flight suit

    Engineers would keep life support, power and machines running. Astronauts spend a lot of their time on maintenance and repairs.

    Where to find them: Water recycler, Power tower, Fabrication shop

  • Pilot

    With a visor and flight gear

    Pilots and rover drivers would move people and cargo safely between landing pads, bases and work sites.

    Where to find them: Spaceport, Pressurized rover, Launch pad

  • Security robot

    Armored helper

    An imagined robot in protective armor. Real robots are mostly used for inspection and dangerous jobs, and people decide what robots are allowed to do.

    Where to find them: Settlement operations center, Robot monument, Civic dome

  • Doctor

    Scientist in a lab coat

    Far from Earth, the crew must handle medical problems themselves, with help from doctors on Earth by video and ultrasound guidance.

    Where to find them: Medical bay, Science lab, Low-gravity gym

  • Helper robot

    Round assistant

    Small robots like the Astrobee free-flyers on the space station take inventory, film experiments and save astronauts' time.

    Where to find them: Robot monument, Hologram hall, Life support dome

  • Cargo robot

    Carrying a container

    Robots that lift and carry would move supplies, ice and parts around the base.

    Where to find them: Robotics factory, Ice mine and water works, Cargo pod

  • Cart robot

    Pushing a supply cart

    Robots that push carts would move heavy equipment so people don't have to.

    Where to find them: Robotic crane, Fabrication shop, Space building materials
