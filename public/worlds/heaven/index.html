This city is imagined. It is inspired mostly by the Bible's pictures of heaven, and by ideas of paradise in other faiths. People picture heaven in many different ways, and some people don't believe in it. Each page marks what is imagined, what faiths teach and what is real today.

How to read these pages

Each page has three kinds of section. Imagined sections describe the artist's city. Belief sections describe what a faith teaches, in its own words where possible. Real world sections describe people, places and science in our world today, with sources.

Gates and welcome

How the city greets everyone who arrives.

  • Pearl gateA gate made of one giant pearl. In the Bible's Book of Revelation, the heavenly city has twelve gates, each made of a single pearl.
  • Welcome hallA hall where every newcomer is welcomed. Many faiths teach that welcoming strangers is one of the best things a person can do.
  • Sky bridgeA golden bridge across the sky. Many traditions picture a bridge or a stairway between the earth and heaven.
  • Cloud islandA little island of marble and cloud floating in the sky, with a waterfall pouring from its edge.

Light and peace

Places of light, prayer and peace.

  • Palace of lightA great golden palace full of light. The Book of Revelation describes a city that needs no sun, because God's glory gives it light.
  • Lamp towerA slim tower with a golden lamp on top, shining over the city all night long.
  • Prayer chapelA small round chapel, warm with light: a quiet place to pray, think or simply be still.
  • Bell towerA tall white tower of golden bells that ring out across the city in celebration.
  • Angel statueA golden angel with open wings on top of a tall column. In Judaism, Christianity and Islam, angels are God's messengers.
  • Dove statueA golden dove with open wings: a picture of peace and new beginnings.

Water, trees and gardens

The river, the trees and the gardens that so many pictures of paradise share.

  • Tree of lifeA huge tree with golden leaves and glowing fruit, its roots in a pool of clear water.
  • Fountain of lifeA marble fountain of sparkling water. Many pictures of paradise have a river or spring of pure, living water at their heart.
  • Spring poolA marble pool fed by a waterfall and shaded by cypress trees: a place to rest by quiet water.
  • Garden of lightA walled garden of glowing lilies, a long pool and cypress trees. The word "paradise" first meant a walled garden.
  • Lily gardenA bed of white lilies and golden flowers. Jesus pointed to the flowers of the field to teach people not to worry.

Sharing and feasts

Where everything is shared and everyone has enough.

  • Banquet hallA long golden hall with a table set for a feast. Many faiths picture heaven as a great meal where everyone is invited.
  • Sharing pavilionA golden pavilion full of fruit and flowers, where everything is free to take and free to give.
  • Hall of treasuresA marble hall overflowing with glowing gold. Jesus taught that the real treasure is goodness, stored up in heaven.
  • Lantern circleA circle of marble benches around a great golden lamp, where people gather in the evening to talk and share stories.
  • Rainbow pavilionAn open pavilion of flowers under a soft rainbow, a sign of promise and peace.

Wisdom and music

Where people keep learning, and the city fills with music.

  • Hall of wisdomA domed library of light where learning never ends, inspired by faiths that treasure wisdom and by the great libraries of today.
  • Scroll libraryA small marble library of scrolls. Some of the oldest scrolls in the world are copies of holy books.
  • School of wisdomA school with a little gold dome and a bell, where people of every age keep learning.
  • Harp workshopA marble workshop where golden harps and trumpets are made for the city's music.

Play and celebration

Running, games and joy.

  • Golden trackAn oval running track with gold lanes around a green field, where every race is run for joy.
  • Victors' arenaA white marble stadium crowned with golden laurel wreaths, where every effort is celebrated.

Homes

A home for everyone.

  • House of many roomsA great white mansion with many wings and gardens. Jesus told his friends that his Father's house has many rooms.
  • Marble cottageA family cottage of white marble with a gold roof, flowering vines and a little garden, where no one is ever afraid.
  • Garden villaA villa built around a courtyard with a fountain: a home where people enjoy the fruit of their own work.

Building the city

The tools and treasures that build it.

  • Light craneA golden crane that lifts marble blocks on a beam of light: the city is always being built.
  • Building materialsMarble blocks, gold bars, pearls and gold tiles: the materials of the heavenly city.

Vehicles and getting around

How people and goods move around the city.

  • Golden chariotA small golden chariot pulled by a white horse. In the Bible, the prophet Elijah is carried up to heaven in a chariot of fire.
  • Cloud carriageA little white carriage with gold trim that floats on a puff of cloud instead of wheels.
  • Swan boatA swan-shaped golden boat with white wings, gliding just above the ground.
  • Donkey with fruitA white donkey carrying baskets of fruit and flowers, led by a smiling boy.
