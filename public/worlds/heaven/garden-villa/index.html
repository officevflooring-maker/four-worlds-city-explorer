Garden villa

Inspired by: Isaiah 65:21

A villa built around a courtyard with a fountain: a home where people enjoy the fruit of their own work.

Imagined The villa

Every room opens onto a sunny courtyard with a fountain, where the family eats, plays and grows its own fruit.

Belief New heavens and a new earth

The prophet Isaiah imagines "new heavens and a new earth" where people "will build houses and dwell in them; they will plant vineyards and eat their fruit" (Isaiah 65:17–21). In this picture, no one loses their home or the fruit of their work.

Real world Courtyard homes

Houses built around a private courtyard are found all over the world, from the Roman domus to the courtyard houses of Beijing and the riads of Fez in Morocco. The courtyard brings light, fresh air and a safe place to play into the middle of the home.

Key words

Courtyard
An open space surrounded by buildings or walls.
Vineyard
A field where grapes are grown.
Riad
A traditional Moroccan house built around a courtyard garden.

Related buildings

  • Garden of lightA walled garden of glowing lilies, a long pool and cypress trees. The word "paradise" first meant a walled garden.
  • Marble cottageA family cottage of white marble with a gold roof, flowering vines and a little garden, where no one is ever afraid.
  • Fountain of lifeA marble fountain of sparkling water. Many pictures of paradise have a river or spring of pure, living water at their heart.

The same idea in other worlds

  • Ancient RomeTown house (domus)A private family house that faced inward, built around open-air halls and gardens.
  • Ancient RomeCountry villaA luxurious country house where wealthy Romans escaped the noise of the city.
  • Modern TownFamily houseA detached house with a porch and garden, built for one household. It is the most common home in many countries.
  • Modern TownSuburban houseA house with a driveway and garage on the edge of a city, designed around travel by car.
  • Elven Green WorldHillside homeA home tucked into a grassy hill. Real earth-sheltered homes use the soil around them to stay cool in summer and warm in winter.
  • Elven Green WorldTimber family houseAn imagined house carved from wood. Real engineered timber now builds homes and towers that store carbon instead of releasing it.
