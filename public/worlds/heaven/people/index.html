Heavenly City · People

People of the Heavenly City

Imagined The people of this city are imagined. The Bible pictures a great crowd "from every nation and tribe and people and tongue" in heaven (Revelation 7:9), where no one is sick, hungry or sad. The pictures show residents, musicians, builders and angels.

  • Woman in white

    A resident of the city

    In the Book of Revelation, a great crowd "from every nation, tribe, people and language" stands in heaven wearing white robes (Revelation 7:9). In this imagined city, everyone who lives here is welcome, at home and at peace.

    Where to find them: Welcome hall, Garden of light, Marble cottage

    Find them on the map

  • Harpist

    Musician with a golden harp

    Music fills the Bible: King David played the lyre and wrote many of the psalms, and Paul and Silas sang hymns to God at midnight in prison (Acts 16:25). Harps and lyres are among the oldest instruments: some found in the royal tombs of Ur, in what is now Iraq, are about 4,500 years old.

    Where to find them: Harp workshop, Bell tower, Lantern circle

    Find them on the map

  • Child with a palm branch

    Waving in celebration

    When Jesus rode into Jerusalem, the crowds took palm branches and went out to meet him (John 12:13), and Revelation pictures a great crowd in heaven holding palm branches (Revelation 7:9). Palm branches are a sign of joy and victory.

    Where to find them: Donkey with fruit, Golden track, Pearl gate

    Find them on the map

  • Angel with a trumpet

    Messenger with good news

    In the Bible, angels are God's messengers: Gabriel told Mary that she would have a son, Jesus, and the Bible says that the Lord will come back "with the trumpet of God" (1 Thessalonians 4:16). This angel is one artist's picture; the Bible describes angels in different ways.

    Where to find them: Angel statue, Bell tower, Cloud carriage

    Find them on the map

  • Elder with a staff

    Wise and welcoming

    The Bible honors older people: "Gray hair is a crown of glory" (Proverbs 16:31). Revelation pictures 24 elders dressed in white around God's throne (Revelation 4:4), and in many families grandparents are the keepers of stories and advice.

    Where to find them: Hall of wisdom, School of wisdom, Scroll library

    Find them on the map · Join their tour: The story of Jesus

  • Woman with golden fruit

    Sharing the harvest

    Fruit appears all through the Bible, from the trees of the garden of Eden (Genesis 2:9) to the tree of life with its twelve kinds of fruit (Revelation 22:2). The Bible says that "the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control" (Galatians 5:22–23).

    Where to find them: Tree of life, Sharing pavilion, Banquet hall

    Find them on the map

  • Angel builder

    Carrying a gold brick

    In this imagined city, even angels help build. Jesus told his friends that he was going to "prepare a place" for them (John 14:2). Building homes with and for other people is one of the most practical kinds of kindness.

    Where to find them: Light crane, Building materials, House of many rooms

    Find them on the map

  • Pearl carrier

    Bringing a pearl for the gates

    In Revelation, each of the city's twelve gates is made of a single pearl (Revelation 21:21). Real pearls grow inside oysters and mussels, layer by layer, around a tiny piece of grit or tissue that got inside.

    Where to find them: Pearl gate, Building materials, Hall of treasures
