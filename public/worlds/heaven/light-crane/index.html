Imagined The crane

Instead of a rope, a beam of soft light holds each glowing marble block and lowers it gently into place.

Belief Rebuilding together

In the Bible, Nehemiah returns to Jerusalem, finds its walls in ruins and calls to the people: "Let us start rebuilding", and they work together to raise the walls again (Nehemiah 2:18). Many faiths teach that people are called to help build a better world, not only to wait for one.

Real world Building after disasters

After floods, storms and earthquakes, volunteers from charities and faith groups often travel to help rebuild homes and schools. Real cranes lift heavy loads with steel cables and pulleys, balanced by heavy counterweights so they don't tip over.

Key words

Crane

Counterweight
A weight that balances a load.
Disaster

The same idea in other worlds

  • Ancient RomeTreadwheel craneA wooden crane with pulleys and a giant wheel that workers walked inside to lift heavy stone blocks.
  • Modern TownTower craneA tall crane that elevators steel, concrete and glass to the upper floors of new buildings.
  • Elven Green WorldLiving craneAn imagined crane woven from vines. In the real world, fast-growing bamboo is strong enough to build scaffolding and houses.
  • Robotic FutureRobotic craneA self-guided crane that lifts modules and soil on the Moon, where things weigh less but are just as hard to stop.
  • Ancient EgyptLifting leverA wooden lever with a counterweight, like the shaduf Egyptians used to lift water. Builders moved giant stones with levers, ramps and sledges.
