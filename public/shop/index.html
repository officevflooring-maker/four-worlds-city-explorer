Good to know

  • Everything is a single payment. No subscriptions and no ads.
  • Payment happens on Etsy, under Etsy's own privacy policy. This site never sees payment details.
  • Etsy emails you a download link, and your files stay on your Etsy Purchases page, so you can download again later.
  • If something doesn't work, you get a full refund within 30 days. Send us a message on Etsy.
