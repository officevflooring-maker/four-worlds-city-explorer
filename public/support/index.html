Support

Help keep it free

The Atlas of Cities is free for everyone, with no ads, accounts or tracking. If it helps your class or your family, you can help it grow.

Support on Patreon

This button opens Patreon, a separate website.

What support pays for

  • New worlds, buildings, vehicles and guided tours.
  • Printable packs and worksheets for classrooms.
  • Fact-checking by teachers and historians.
  • Hosting, so the site stays fast and free.

Before you give

  • Donations are optional. Everything on the site stays free either way.
  • Payments and personal details are handled by Patreon, under Patreon's own privacy policy. This site never sees them.
  • Under 18? Please ask a parent or guardian first.

Free ways to help

  • Share the site with a teacher, school or family.
  • Use the illustrations in your lessons and projects, with the credit "Illustration: The Atlas of Cities".
