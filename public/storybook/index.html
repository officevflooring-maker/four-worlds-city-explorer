Questions

Are these the stories on this site?

Yes. The stories are free to read here. The Storybook puts all seven in one book to print, with every painting on its own page, a contents page and a page for your own story.

What age is it for?

Ages 4 to 9 for reading aloud. Confident readers can read it by themselves.

Do I need a color printer?

It looks best in color, since every page is a painting. Print the stories one at a time if you like.

Can I print more copies?

Yes, for your own home or classroom. Please don't share the file online.
