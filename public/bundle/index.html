Questions

What do I get?

A one-page PDF with the link to your download page. There you download all 15 printables as PDFs, each in US Letter and A4: 425 pages in all.

Why a link, and not the files themselves?

Together the files are too big to attach to one order. Your download page has them all in one place, and you can come back to it whenever you like.

Are these the same files as the single products?

Yes, exactly the same PDFs. The pack only costs less.

Can I print more copies?

Yes, for your own home or one classroom. Please don't share the files or your download link online.

Is Life RPG in the bundle?

No. Life RPG is an app with its own key, so it is sold on its own.

What about printables you add later?

The bundle holds the 15 printables listed on this page.
