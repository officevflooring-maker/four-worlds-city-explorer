Memory match

Turn over two cards at a time and find all six pairs. Choose a game:

    How to play

    Turn over two cards at a time. If they make a pair, they stay face up; if not, they turn back. Find all six pairs in as few moves as you can.

    What you learn

    Buildings that do the same job in different worlds, and the names of the places in each city.
