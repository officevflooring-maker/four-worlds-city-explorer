Zoom detective

Each picture starts as a close-up and slowly zooms out. Name it early for 3 points, a little later for 2, or at the end for 1. Choose a game:

Read about them

    How to play

    A close-up picture slowly zooms out. Pick the building's name as soon as you know it: early answers score 3 points, later ones 2, and answers at the end 1.

    What you learn

    Noticing details, such as roofs, columns, windows and colors, that tell one building from another.
