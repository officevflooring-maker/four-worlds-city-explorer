      How to play

      You and the computer each get five cards. Every round names a stat (Strength, Wisdom or Heart) and a home world. Pick a card: the higher number wins the round, and a card from the home world gets 2 extra. Win more of the five rounds, then keep one of the computer's cards for your album.

      What you learn

      Which world each person, animal, vehicle and landmark belongs to, and how to plan ahead: a strong card used now is gone for the next round.
