World sort

Pictures pop up one at a time. Tap where each one belongs, as fast as you can, before the minute is up. Choose a game:

On a keyboard, press the number on a button.

Take another look

    How to play

    A picture pops up: tap the world it belongs to, or in the second game, whether it is from long ago, today, a possible future or an imagined world. Sort as many as you can in one minute. On a keyboard, the number keys work too.

    What you learn

    Telling history, today, the future and imagined worlds apart, by looking closely at buildings, people and vehicles.
