Picture puzzle

Choose how many pieces, then a picture. The pieces get mixed up: tap two pieces to swap them, or drag one onto another, until the picture is whole again.

Read about it

How to play

Choose 9, 16 or 25 pieces, then a picture. Tap two pieces to swap them, or drag one onto another. A piece in the right place locks, and Peek shows the whole picture for a moment.

What you learn

Seeing how parts make a whole, planning ahead and patience, with a close look at a landmark from each world.
