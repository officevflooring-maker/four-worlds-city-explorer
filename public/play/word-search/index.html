Word search

Find ten hidden city words. They read across, down or diagonally. Tap the first letter, then the last.

    How to play

    Find the ten hidden words. They read across, down or diagonally, never backwards. Tap the first letter of a word, then the last, or drag across it. Each word you find shows what it means.

    What you learn

    The key words of city life in six worlds, with a short meaning for every one.
