Odd one out

Four pictures, and one of them does not belong. Can you spot it? Ten puzzles a game:

Read about them

    How to play

    Four pictures appear. Three of them belong together and one does not: tap the odd one out, then read why. Ten puzzles make a game.

    What you learn

    Comparing and grouping: telling which world a place comes from, and what job a building does for its city.
