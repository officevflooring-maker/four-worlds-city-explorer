Card memory

Turn over two cards at a time and find the seven pairs of city cards.

    How to play

    Turn over two cards at a time. If they show the same world, they stay face up; if not, they turn back. Find all seven pairs in as few moves as you can.

    What you learn

    The seven city worlds of this site, and a better memory: try to remember where each card was.
