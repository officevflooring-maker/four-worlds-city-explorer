Life RPG

Turn real-life goals into quests. Finish them to earn XP, level up and grow your own city.

Coming soon · $3, one time

How it works

  • Pick a few quests for today: a workout, homework, reading, chores or a step toward a goal.
  • Tap a quest when it's done to earn XP. Levels, skills and badges follow.
  • Every quest builds part of your city, and milestones add landmarks.
  • Take on a boss battle: one hard thing you've been putting off.
  • Several players can share one device, with a family scoreboard.

Good to know

  • One payment of $3. No subscription and no ads.
  • No account. Progress stays on your device, and you can save a backup file.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers, and offline once it's open.
  • Starter quests for adults and for teens. You can change any of them.
  • A grown-up buys it, on our store's website. This site never sees payment details.
