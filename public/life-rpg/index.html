Questions

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay $5 once, and it's yours. There are no ads and nothing else to buy.

What do I need?

A phone, tablet or computer with an up-to-date web browser. You can add Life RPG to your home screen, where it opens like an app.

Do I need an account?

No. Your receipt email has a key. Type it in once on each device you play on, up to 10 devices.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device, not on a server, so it stays private. Save a backup file now and then, and open it on another device to carry on there.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes, once it has opened. Only unlocking a new device needs a connection.

Can I change the quests?

Yes. Every quest, reward and stat can be changed. The starter quests cover work, family, health, money, learning and faith; remove any you don't want. There is a teen starter pack with school, chores and sports.

Can kids play?

Yes, with a grown-up. A grown-up buys it, and several players can share one device.
