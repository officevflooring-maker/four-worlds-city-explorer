How does a city work?

Explore four illustrated cities, from ancient Rome to a robotic future. Discover what each building does, compare how each world solves the same problems, and think about what makes a good place to live.

How to use this site

  1. Look around. Open a world and select any of its 140 buildings to read its own page.
  2. Look closer. Every page has photos of real places, things to notice and questions to talk about.
  3. Compare. See how all four worlds handle water, gathering, travel and nature.

For families and educators

Free to use, with no sign-up, ads or tracking. Questions are open-ended and work well for discussion at home or in class. Every building page lists the sources its facts are based on, and marks clearly what is real, what is imagined and what is a possible future.
