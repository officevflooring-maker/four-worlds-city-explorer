Robotic Future · Guided tour

Human choices in robotic cities

Technology, energy and who decides.

Stops
7
Time
20 to 30 minutes
Grades
5-12

Tour stops

  1. 1. Power tower
  2. 2. Water recycler
  3. 3. Robot monument
  4. 4. Cargo pod
  5. 5. Robotics factory
  6. 6. Settlement operations center
  7. 7. Civic dome
  8. Think it over

  1. Stop 1

    Power tower

    Everything here needs power, all the time. Solar works by day; small nuclear reactors could work through the long lunar night.

    What is the most important thing to keep powered in an emergency?

    Read the full page about the power tower

  2. Stop 2

    Water recycler

    We recycle almost all our water, as astronauts on the space station already do.

    What could your home recycle that it doesn't now?

    Read the full page about the water recycler

  3. Stop 3

    Robot monument

    Small robots take inventory and inspect equipment, saving people time.

    Which jobs would you give to a robot? Which would you keep?

    Read the full page about the robot monument

  4. Stop 4

    Cargo pod

    Self-driving pods carry ice and supplies. Giant self-driving trucks already work in mines on Earth.

    Who is responsible if a self-driving vehicle makes a mistake?

    Read the full page about the cargo pod

  5. Stop 5

    Robotics factory

    Robots build parts from local materials, so less has to be shipped from Earth.

    What happens to people's jobs when robots can build things?

    Read the full page about the robotics factory

  6. Stop 6

    Settlement operations center

    People watch over everything from here and step in when something goes wrong.

    Should a robot ever make a decision without asking a person?

    Read the full page about the settlement operations center

  7. Stop 7

    Civic dome

    Who makes the rules in a settlement? Real treaties already set some rules for space.

    How should a small settlement make fair decisions?

    Read the full page about the civic dome

Think it over

  1. Would you want to live in this settlement? Why or why not?
  2. What should robots never be allowed to do?
  3. Who should decide how new technology is used: experts, companies or everyone?
