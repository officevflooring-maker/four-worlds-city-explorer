The story of Jesus

The good news of Jesus, from beginning to end.

Stops
10
Time
20 to 30 minutes
Grades
2-8

Tour stops

  1. 1. Tree of life
  2. 2. Welcome hall
  3. 3. Dove statue
  4. 4. Fountain of life
  5. 5. Sharing pavilion
  6. 6. Donkey with fruit
  7. 7. Banquet hall
  8. 8. Sky bridge
  9. 9. Garden of light
  10. 10. Pearl gate
  11. Think it over

  1. Stop 1

    Tree of life

    We start where the Bible starts. God made a good world, but people turned away from him, and they lost the tree of life.

    Why do you think God did not give up on the people he made?

    Read the full page about the tree of life · Find it on the map

  2. Stop 2

    Welcome hall

    Here there is a bed for everyone, but in Bethlehem there was no room. God's Son was born where animals were fed.

    Why might God's Son have been born in such a humble place?

    Read the full page about the welcome hall · Find it on the map

  3. Stop 3

    Dove statue

    At the Jordan River, the Spirit came down on Jesus like a dove, and a voice from heaven said, "This is My beloved Son."

    What did God say about Jesus that day?

    Read the full page about the dove statue · Find it on the map

  4. Stop 4

    Fountain of life

    At a well, Jesus offered a woman living water. Whoever drinks the water he gives, he said, will never thirst.

    What do you think "living water" means?

    Read the full page about the fountain of life · Find it on the map

  5. Stop 5

    Sharing pavilion

    A boy shared five loaves and two fish, and Jesus fed five thousand men. "I am the bread of life," he said.

    What small thing could you share?

    Read the full page about the sharing pavilion · Find it on the map

  6. Stop 6

    Donkey with fruit

    Crowds waved palm branches as Jesus rode into Jerusalem on a donkey, a gentle King.

    Why do you think Jesus came on a donkey and not a war horse?

    Read the full page about the donkey with fruit · Find it on the map

  7. Stop 7

    Banquet hall

    At the last supper Jesus washed his friends' feet and shared bread and wine. "Love one another," he said.

    What does it look like to serve others?

    Read the full page about the banquet hall · Find it on the map

  8. Stop 8

    Sky bridge

    Jesus died on a cross. The Bible says he died for our sins, to bring us to God, like a bridge.

    What did Jesus pray for the people who hurt him?

    Read the full page about the sky bridge · Find it on the map

  9. Stop 9

    Garden of light

    On the third day the tomb was empty. In the garden, Mary heard Jesus call her name. He is risen!

    Why is the empty tomb good news?

    Read the full page about the garden of light · Find it on the map

  10. Stop 10

    Pearl gate

    We end at a gate that is never shut. The Bible's last page says God will make all things new, and invites everyone: "Come!"

    What would you like to tell someone about this story?

    Read the full page about the pearl gate · Find it on the map

Think it over

  1. Which part of the story of Jesus would you like to read again?
  2. Why do you think this story is called "good news"?
  3. Read the whole story, part by part, starting at the Tree of life.
