Heavenly City · Guided tour

A city of goodness

Welcome, sharing, learning and peace.

Stops
8
Time
20 to 30 minutes
Grades
3-9

Tour stops

  1. 1. Pearl gate
  2. 2. Welcome hall
  3. 3. Fountain of life
  4. 4. House of many rooms
  5. 5. Banquet hall
  6. 6. Sharing pavilion
  7. 7. Hall of wisdom
  8. 8. Rainbow pavilion
  9. Think it over

  1. Stop 1

    Pearl gate

    We start at a gate made of one giant pearl. In the Bible's Book of Revelation, the heavenly city has twelve gates, each made of a single pearl.

    What makes a place feel open to everyone?

    Read the full page about the pearl gate · Find it on the map

  2. Stop 2

    Welcome hall

    Every newcomer is welcomed here. Many faiths teach that welcoming strangers is one of the best things a person can do.

    How could your school or town welcome someone new?

    Read the full page about the welcome hall · Find it on the map

  3. Stop 3

    Fountain of life

    Many pictures of paradise have a river or spring of pure, living water at their heart. Here it is free for everyone.

    Who in our world still has no clean water nearby? What could change that?

    Read the full page about the fountain of life · Find it on the map

  4. Stop 4

    House of many rooms

    Jesus told his friends that his Father's house has many rooms. In this city there is room for everyone.

    What would it take for everyone in your town to have a home?

    Read the full page about the house of many rooms · Find it on the map

  5. Stop 5

    Banquet hall

    Many faiths picture heaven as a great meal where everyone is invited. No one is left out of the table.

    Who might be missing from the table in your town?

    Read the full page about the banquet hall · Find it on the map

  6. Stop 6

    Sharing pavilion

    Everything here is free to take and free to give. In our world, food banks and libraries share in the same spirit.

    What could your family or class share with others?

    Read the full page about the sharing pavilion · Find it on the map

  7. Stop 7

    Hall of wisdom

    A library of light, where learning never ends.

    What would you like to keep learning about all your life?

    Read the full page about the hall of wisdom · Find it on the map

  8. Stop 8

    Rainbow pavilion

    We end under a rainbow, a sign of promise and peace. In the Bible, the rainbow is the sign of God's promise to Noah.

    What promise could you make to help your town be a kinder place?

    Read the full page about the rainbow pavilion · Find it on the map

Think it over

  1. Which stop showed an idea your town could use right now?
  2. People of many faiths, and people of none, share many of these hopes. Which ones?
  3. What would make your neighborhood a place of goodness and happiness?

For a full lesson, see What makes a good place to live? Discussion questions.
