What is solarpunk?

Grades
7-12
Time
10-minute read
Subjects
English, art, environmental science

Quick answer

Solarpunk is an art, writing and design movement that imagines a hopeful future where cities run on clean energy, are full of plants and are shaped by their communities. It mixes real green technology with imagination and asks: what future do we want to build?

Where it came from

Solarpunk grew online in the 2010s as a reaction to gloomy science fiction full of ruined, polluted cities. Artists and writers began imagining futures where people solved problems together.

Real technologies in solarpunk worlds

  • Solar panels, wind and geothermal energy.
  • Green roofs, living walls and vertical forests.
  • Rain gardens and wetlands that clean water.
  • Repair cafes, community gardens and shared tools.
  • Walkable, bike-friendly streets and public transit.

Creative prompts for class

  • Write a day in the life of a teenager in your town in 2075, solarpunk style.
  • Redesign one street near school as a solarpunk street. Label every feature.
  • Pick one building from the Elven world on this site and write how it could work in a real city.
  • Debate: is solarpunk realistic, or just a nice dream? What would it take?

Questions students and teachers ask

What does solarpunk mean?

A genre and movement that imagines a sustainable, community-focused future powered by clean energy and filled with nature.

Is solarpunk real?

It is imaginative, but many of its ideas, such as green roofs, solar power and community gardens, already exist today.
