Answer key: Elven Green World key words

  1. C Walkway: A raised path or bridge. (Canopy climbing course)
  2. G Mycelium: The network of thread-like roots of a fungus. (Mushroom cottage)
  3. A Green roof: A roof covered with growing plants. (Green-roof cottage)
  4. H Turbine: A wheel turned by flowing water, wind or steam. (Crystal waterfall)
  5. J Ecosystem engineer: An animal that changes its habitat in big ways. (Deer shrine)
  6. F Citizen science: Research that anyone can help with. (Seasons tower)
  7. D District heating: Heating many buildings from one shared source. (Warm gathering circle)
  8. I Parasol: A shade, like an umbrella. (Canopy market)
  9. E Biophilia: A love of, or need for, the natural world. (Leafy cottage)
  10. B Long-term: Over a long time, such as decades. (Palace of the long future)
