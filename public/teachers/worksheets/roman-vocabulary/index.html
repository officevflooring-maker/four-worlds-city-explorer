Answer key: Ancient Rome key words

  1. J Auriga: A chariot driver. (Chariot)
  2. E Story: One floor of a building. (Apartment block (insula))
  3. F Caldarium: The hot room. (Public baths)
  4. B Rostra: The speakers' platform in the Roman Forum. (Forum)
  5. C Gate: An opening in a wall that can be closed. (City walls and towers)
  6. D Dacia: An ancient kingdom in what is now Romania. (Victory column)
  7. I Senate: A council of leading citizens (from senex, old man). (Senate house (curia))
  8. A Battlements: The notched top of a wall for defenders to shelter behind. (City walls and towers)
  9. G Travertine: A hard, pale limestone. (Building materials)
  10. H Axle: The rod that joins two wheels. (Ox cart)
