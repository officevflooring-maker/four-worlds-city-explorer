Answer key: Robotic Future key words

  1. C Governance: How decisions are made and rules enforced. (Civic dome)
  2. H Radiation: Energy that travels as waves or fast particles. (Radiation storm shelter)
  3. J Far side: The side of the Moon that always faces away from Earth. (Far-side radio telescope)
  4. B Simulant: A material made on Earth to copy lunar or Martian soil. (Space building materials)
  5. E Levitation: Floating in the air without support. (Hover car)
  6. A Expandable habitat: A home that is launched folded and inflated later. (Pod home)
  7. D Planetary protection: Preventing contamination between worlds. (Science lab)
  8. I Artificial gravity: A force that feels like gravity, created by spinning or speeding up. (Spinning gravity arena)
  9. G Solar flare: A sudden burst of energy from the Sun. (Space weather station)
  10. F Counterweight: A weight that balances another. (Space elevator anchor)
