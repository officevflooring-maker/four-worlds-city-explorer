  • Hieratic: A quick, joined-up form of Egyptian writing.
  • Inherit: To receive property from someone who has died.
  • Relief: A carving that stands out from a flat surface.
  • Marsh: Low, wet land where reeds grow.
  • Deben: An Egyptian weight, about 91 grams, used to measure value.
  • Remedy: A medicine or treatment.
  • Season: One of the parts of the year.
  • Migrant: A person who moves to live in another place.
  • Expedition: A long journey made for a purpose.
  • Capital: The top part of a column.
