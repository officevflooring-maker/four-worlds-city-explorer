  • Promise: Saying you will certainly do something.
  • Scroll: A long roll of paper or parchment with writing on it.
  • Mansion: A very large house.
  • Belonging: Feeling part of a group.
  • Basin: A wide bowl that holds water.
  • Laurel: A tree whose leaves were made into victory wreaths.
  • Hope: The feeling that good things can happen.
  • Paradise: A place of perfect happiness; first, a walled garden.
  • Wreath: A ring of leaves or flowers.
  • Fountain: A structure that sends out a stream of water.
