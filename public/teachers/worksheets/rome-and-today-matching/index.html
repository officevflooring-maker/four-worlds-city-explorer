Answer key: Rome and today

  1. 1 → D: Records office (tabularium) and Public library both keep books and records.
  2. 2 → A: Apartment block (insula) and Residential tower both house many families close together.
  3. 3 → F: Basilica and Town hall both are where leaders meet and make decisions.
  4. 4 → C: Street shop (taberna) and Shops with homes above both supply things people need.
  5. 5 → E: Forum and Public square and fountain both are open spaces where people meet.
  6. 6 → B: Public baths and Health clinic both look after people's health.
