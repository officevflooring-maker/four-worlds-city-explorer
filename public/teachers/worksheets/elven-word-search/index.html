  • Overpass: A bridge that crosses over a road.
  • Wellbeing: Feeling healthy and happy.
  • Watershed: All the land that drains into one river or lake.
  • Footprint: The area of ground a building covers.
  • Enzyme: A molecule that speeds up a chemical reaction.
  • Rewilding: Letting nature restore itself, often by bringing back key species.
  • Wetland: Land covered with shallow water, like a marsh.
  • Parasol: A shade, like an umbrella.
  • Runner: The smooth strip a sled slides on.
  • Canopy: The top layer of a forest, formed by branches and leaves.
