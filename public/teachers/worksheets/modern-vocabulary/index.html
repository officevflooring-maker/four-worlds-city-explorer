Answer key: Modern Town key words

  1. H Sightline: A clear view from a seat to the field. (Stadium)
  2. G Prevention: Stopping illness before it starts. (Health clinic)
  3. E Primary care: Everyday health care, usually the first place people go. (Health clinic)
  4. D Heat pump: A machine that moves heat from one place to another. (Older brick house)
  5. J Hybrid work: Splitting time between home and the workplace. (Office building)
  6. B Pile: A long column driven into the ground to support a building. (Residential tower)
  7. I Insulation: Material that slows heat moving in or out. (Family house)
  8. C Curtain wall: A non-structural outer wall, often of glass. (Office building)
  9. F Recirculating: Water that is pumped round and reused. (Public square and fountain)
  10. A Heritage: Traditions and history passed down through generations. (Cultural center)
