Answer key: Earth and Mars

  1. 1 → F: Sports field and Low-gravity gym both help people stay fit.
  2. 2 → B: Shops with homes above and Fabrication shop both supply things people need.
  3. 3 → E: Community garden and Greenhouse dome both grow plants.
  4. 4 → A: Science center and observatory and Far-side radio telescope both help people study the world.
  5. 5 → D: Public square and fountain and Community plaza both are open spaces where people meet.
  6. 6 → C: Apartment building and Moon base home both house many families close together.
