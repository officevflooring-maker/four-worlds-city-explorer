Answer key: Earth and Mars

  1. 1 → F: Health clinic and Medical bay both look after people's health.
  2. 2 → B: Science center and observatory and Far-side radio telescope both help people study the world.
  3. 3 → E: Warehouse and Spaceport both store goods.
  4. 4 → A: Sports field and Low-gravity gym both help people stay fit.
  5. 5 → D: Apartment building and Moon base home both house many families close together.
  6. 6 → C: Stadium and Spinning gravity arena both hold shows and games for big crowds.
