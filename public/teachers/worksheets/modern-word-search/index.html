  • Wildfire: An uncontrolled fire in nature.
  • Exhibit: Something displayed for people to explore.
  • Payload: How much weight a vehicle can carry.
  • Barcode: Lines a scanner reads to identify a product.
  • Permit: Official permission to do something, like build.
  • Suburb: A mainly residential area on the edge of a city.
  • Core: The strong central part holding elevators and stairs.
  • Insulation: Material that slows heat moving in or out.
  • Council: A group of elected people who make local decisions.
  • Inclusive: Designed so everyone can join in.
