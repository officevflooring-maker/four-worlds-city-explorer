  • Journeyman: A trained worker paid by the day.
  • Wagon: A vehicle with four wheels; a cart has two.
  • Jetty: An upper floor that sticks out over the one below.
  • League: A group that joins together for a shared purpose.
  • Bronze: A metal made from copper and tin.
  • College: A place where students live and study together.
  • Treadwheel: A wheel turned by people walking inside it.
  • Lists: The fenced field where a tournament took place.
  • Woolsack: The wool-stuffed seat of the Lord Speaker in the House of Lords.
  • Drawbridge: A bridge that can be raised.
