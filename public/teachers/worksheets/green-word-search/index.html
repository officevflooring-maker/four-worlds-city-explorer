  • Turbine: A wheel turned by flowing water, wind or steam.
  • Stress: The body's response to pressure or worry.
  • Certified: Checked to meet a standard.
  • Commons: Resources shared by a group.
  • Migration: Animals traveling between places with the seasons.
  • Gene: Instructions inside a cell that shape how a living thing grows.
  • Watershed: All the land that drains into one river or lake.
  • Rewilding: Letting nature restore itself, often by bringing back key species.
  • Mycelium: The root-like threads of fungi.
  • Reflect: Bounce back.
