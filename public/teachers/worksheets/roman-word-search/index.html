  • Triumph: A victory parade for a Roman general.
  • Aisle: A side passage separated by columns.
  • Hearth: A fireplace, the center of a home.
  • Forum: A town's main public square.
  • Castellum: A building where incoming water was divided into pipes.
  • Stylus: A pointed tool for writing on wax.
  • Gilded: Covered with a thin layer of gold.
  • Podium: The high platform a temple stands on.
  • Axle: The rod that joins two wheels.
  • Scroll: A long rolled-up sheet used as a book.
