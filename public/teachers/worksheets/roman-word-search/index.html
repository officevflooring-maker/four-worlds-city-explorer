  • Lanista: The owner or manager of a troupe of gladiators.
  • Press: A machine for squeezing out liquid.
  • Epitaph: Words written on a tomb.
  • Gilded: Covered with a thin layer of gold.
  • Dolium: A large clay jar; snack bars set them into their counters (plural dolia).
  • Senator: A member of the Senate.
  • Millstone: A heavy stone used to grind grain.
  • Mausoleum: A large, grand tomb.
  • Quaestor: An official in charge of public money.
  • Atrium: The main hall, open to the sky.
