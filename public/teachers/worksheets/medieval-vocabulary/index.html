Answer key: Medieval Kingdom key words

  1. A Fallow: Plowed land left unplanted for a season to rest. (Farmstead)
  2. D Hearth: The floor of a fireplace, or a fire on the floor. (Peasant cottage)
  3. J Carrier: A person who carries goods for others, for a fee. (Covered wagon)
  4. C Battlements: The notched top of a wall that protected defenders. (Royal castle)
  5. F Lists: The fenced field where a tournament took place. (Tournament field)
  6. I Portcullis: A heavy grille that slides down to block a gateway. (Gatehouse)
  7. G Undercroft: A cellar under a building, often vaulted. (Merchant's house)
  8. H Timber-framed: Built with a frame of wooden beams. (Town house)
  9. B Cider: A drink made from pressed apples. (Orchard)
  10. E Course: One part of a meal, served at one time. (Feast hall)
