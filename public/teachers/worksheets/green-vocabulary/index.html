Answer key: Green World key words

  1. E Commissioner: A person given a special public duty. (Palace of the long future)
  2. C Shielded light: A lamp covered so that light shines down. (Star dome)
  3. J Walkway: A raised path or bridge. (Canopy climbing course)
  4. H Cargo bike: A bicycle built to carry loads. (Flower cart)
  5. G Watershed: All the land that drains into one river or lake. (Spring circle)
  6. D Heatwave: A period of unusually hot weather. (Canopy market)
  7. B Reservoir: A lake used to store water. (Spring circle)
  8. I Planter: A container for growing plants. (Tree tower home)
  9. F Gene: Instructions inside a cell that shape how a living thing grows. (Firefly tower)
  10. A Monitoring: Checking what is happening over time. (Council terrace)
