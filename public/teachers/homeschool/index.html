Homeschool

Free homeschool resources

Six illustrated cities to explore, from ancient Egypt and Rome to a modern town and a settlement on Mars, with lesson guides, printables and games. All free for homeschool families and co-ops, with no sign-up, ads or tracking.

  • Most of it suits ages 8 to 14, and the Bible lesson starts at kindergarten.
  • Free to print and share, for one family or a whole co-op.
  • The Heavenly City tells the story of Jesus, as the Bible tells it.

A week with one city

Spend a week in one city, about 30 to 45 minutes a day.

  1. Monday: exploreOpen one city and read three places your child picks: Ancient Egypt, Ancient Rome, Modern Town, Green World, Robotic Future, Heavenly City.
  2. Tuesday: take the tourOne of the city's people walks you through it, with a question at every stop, and Listen reads it aloud. Guided tours
  3. Wednesday: a lessonPick a guide below for the city's big idea, like how the pyramids were built or how a town gets clean water.
  4. Thursday: printThe city's vocabulary sheet and word search, with answer keys. Worksheets
  5. Friday: playA quiz, a map hunt or the city builder, then count the week's passport stamps. Games

Questions homeschool parents ask

Is it free for homeschool families?

Yes. Every page, lesson guide, worksheet, tour and game is free, with no account. The shop sells optional printable games and packs, and everything else stays free.

What ages is it for?

Most of it suits ages 8 to 14 (grades 3 to 8). Each lesson guide lists its grades: the Bible lesson starts at kindergarten, and a few guides go up to grade 12.

Does my child need an account or an email?

No. Nothing asks for a name or an email. Passport stamps, game scores and the city your child builds stay in your own browser.

Can we use it in a co-op?

Yes. The lesson guides and worksheets are free to print and share for teaching, for one family or a whole group. Please credit The Atlas of Cities.

Is there Bible teaching?

Yes. The Heavenly City tells the story of Jesus as the Bible tells it, in 35 parts from place to place, with verses from the Berean Standard Bible. The Bible lesson guide turns it into a six-week plan.

Does it work on a tablet or a Chromebook?

Yes. It runs in any web browser, on phones, tablets, Chromebooks and computers, with nothing to install.

Teachers Teaching in a public or private school? The same guides list grade ranges and class periods: all teacher guides.
