How it works

  1. 1Print and cut. Ten cards to a page, one page for each world.

  3. 3Plan and write. Fill in the story planner, then write and draw on the writing page.

Questions

What grades is it for?

Grades 2 to 6. Younger writers can draw first and write a sentence or two; older ones can turn a card into a whole story or report.

Does it go with the Classroom Pack?

Yes. Every place on the cards has a fact card in the Classroom Pack, so students can look things up as they write.

Can I copy it for my students?

Yes, as many copies as your class needs. One purchase is for one teacher's classroom or one home; please don't share the file online.
