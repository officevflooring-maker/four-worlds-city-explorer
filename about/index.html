About this project

Four Worlds City Explorer is a free educational and creative website. It uses illustrated cities to help visitors notice how cities work and think about what makes a good place to live.

What is real and what is imagined

Ancient Rome is a historical setting. Its history notes are short, separated from the artist's interpretation, and linked to their sources. The pictures themselves are illustrations, not exact reconstructions.

The modern town, green elven world and robotic future are imagined design settings. They don't describe real places, real cultures or predictions.

Privacy

This site has no accounts, forms, comments, chat, ads, analytics or tracking. It sets no cookies and loads nothing from other websites. Your web host may keep standard server logs.

Accessibility

Every building is listed in text as well as on the map. The site works with a keyboard and screen readers, respects reduced-motion settings, and has no autoplay or flashing effects.

Artwork and reuse

The artwork on this site is not public domain and is not free to reuse. You're welcome to view it and use these pages in class. Please ask before copying or republishing the images. A browsable art collection with clear reuse terms is coming later.
